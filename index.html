Real Estate Immobilier WhatsApp NocoDB GPT-4o Cloudinary

AI Real Estate Lead
Qualification Agent

Agent IA de Qualification
de Leads Immobiliers

Elite Immo — WhatsApp Automation

The situation

La situation

Agents burned 3+ hours daily answering the same WhatsApp questions about price, zone, and availability. After 6pm, high-intent leads hit an offline team and disappeared. Property photos could not be served through WhatsApp because images sat on private Amazon S3 storage — an unsolved bottleneck.

Les agents brulaient 3h+ par jour a repondre aux memes questions WhatsApp. Apres 18h, les leads a forte intention tombaient dans le vide. Les photos de biens ne pouvaient pas etre envoyees via WhatsApp car les images etaient sur Amazon S3 prive — un blocage non resolu.

What changed

Ce qui a change

Automated qualification runs 24/7. Prospects receive photos, pricing, and descriptions in under 3 seconds. All qualified leads auto-logged to Google Sheets. Zero manual effort per inquiry. Zero missed lead.

Qualification automatique 24h/24. Les prospects recoivent photos, prix et descriptions en moins de 3 secondes. Tous les leads qualifies enregistres automatiquement. Zero travail manuel. Zero lead manque.

How it works

Comment ca marche

1

WHAPI Webhook — captures every WhatsApp message. Text, voice (Whisper transcription), and images (GPT-4o Vision) all processed and routed to the correct branch.

Webhook WHAPI — capture chaque message WhatsApp. Texte, audio (Whisper), images (GPT-4o Vision) traites et routes vers la bonne branche.

2

GPT-4o Agent + NocoDB — collects 3 criteria via conversational memory, then queries the live catalog. Strict anti-hallucination rules: only verified listings are shown.

Agent GPT-4o + NocoDB — collecte 3 criteres via memoire conversationnelle, interroge le catalogue en temps reel. Anti-hallucination stricte.

3

Cloudinary Bridge — n8n authenticates with the NocoDB API, downloads the binary from S3, re-uploads to Cloudinary, generating a public URL WhatsApp can render. No 403 errors, no workarounds.

Bridge Cloudinary — n8n s'authentifie NocoDB, telecharge le binaire S3, transfere vers Cloudinary, genere une URL publique affichable sur WhatsApp. Sans erreur 403.

4

Google Sheets CRM — name, WhatsApp number, property type, zone, budget, and status auto-appended the moment a lead qualifies.

CRM Google Sheets — nom, numero, type de bien, zone, budget et statut enregistres automatiquement a la qualification.

& Triage AI

IA Clinique — Prise de RDV

Clinique Divine Misericorde — Patient Automation

The situation

La situation

Reception was overwhelmed handling WhatsApp bookings outside business hours. Symptom assessment was inconsistent. Appointment reminders were sent manually and frequently forgotten. Patients had to repeat their full history every conversation because nothing persisted between sessions.

L'accueil etait submerge par les reservations WhatsApp hors horaires. Le triage des symptomes etait aleatoire. Les rappels de RDV envoyes manuellement et souvent oublies. Les patients devaient tout repeter a chaque conversation.

What changed

Ce qui a change

Reception workload cut by approximately 80%. Critical cases triaged and escalated before the patient arrives. No-show rate dropped with automated 30-minute WhatsApp reminders. Full booking loop completed in under 2 minutes. Zero human intervention required.

Charge de travail accueil reduite de 80% environ. Cas critiques tries avant l'arrivee du patient. Taux d'absence reduit grace aux rappels automatiques. Cycle complet en moins de 2 minutes. Zero intervention humaine.

How it works

Comment ca marche

1

Persistent Patient Memory (Supabase) — full profile stored per patient. Returning patients are auto-detected. The last 15 messages feed AI context. Patients are never asked to repeat themselves.

Memoire Patient (Supabase) — profil complet stocke. Patients reconnus automatiquement. Les 15 derniers messages alimentent le contexte IA.

2

Adaptive Triage Engine — 3 to 4 adaptive questions per symptom type. Urgency scored from low to critical. Staff alerted instantly for high-urgency cases.

Moteur de Triage Adaptatif — 3 a 4 questions selon le symptome. Urgence evaluee de faible a critique. Personnel alerte immediatement pour les cas graves.

3

Smart Calendar Scheduler — real-time Google Calendar availability checked. Three free slots proposed. Patient confirms one. Event created instantly with motif and contact details embedded.

Planificateur Calendar — disponibilites verifiees en temps reel. 3 creneaux proposes, patient confirme, evenement cree avec motif et coordonnees.

4

30-min Cron Reminder — scheduler runs every 5 minutes. Fetches appointments in the 25–35 minute window. WhatsApp sent to patient and notification to clinic.

Cron de Rappel 30 min — toutes les 5 minutes. Recupere les RDV dans la fenetre 25–35 min. WhatsApp patient + notification clinique.

FR Immo — D+2 / D+7 / D+14 Email Automation

The situation

La situation

70% of post-visit prospects were lost to zero follow-up. Agents had no bandwidth to write personalized emails. Hot leads went cold within 48 hours. Closed deals kept receiving re-engagement emails, creating an unprofessional impression.

70% des prospects apres visite disparaissaient faute de suivi. Les agents n'avaient pas le temps d'ecrire des emails personnalises. Les leads chauds refroidissaient en 48h. Les deals fermes recevaient encore des relances — peu professionnel.

What changed

Ce qui a change

Three fully personalized emails per prospect, sent at D+2, D+7, and D+14. Agents notified only when a human response is needed. Zero emails written manually. Closed deals auto-excluded. Sequence runs indefinitely.

3 emails entierement personnalises par prospect a J+2, J+7 et J+14. Agents notifies uniquement quand l'humain est requis. Zero email manuel. Deals fermes exclus automatiquement.

How it works

Comment ca marche

1

Tally Form trigger — agent fills a post-visit form. Data written to Google Sheets in real time. Only prospects with status "Undecided" enter the nurturing sequence.

Declencheur Tally — formulaire post-visite. Donnees ecrites dans Google Sheets en temps reel. Seuls les prospects "Indecis" entrent dans la sequence.

2

Gemini 2.5 Flash copywriting — D+2: warm check-in, no pressure. D+7: three practical buying tips, property subtly referenced. D+14: gentle urgency with a 10-minute call offer.

Redaction Gemini 2.5 — J+2: prise de nouvelles sans pression. J+7: 3 conseils pratiques. J+14: urgence douce + offre d'appel 10 minutes.

3

Smart Status Gate — before every send, the workflow re-reads the Sheets row. If status is "Closed," the entire remaining sequence stops. No re-engagement emails sent to existing clients.

Porte de Statut Intelligente — avant chaque envoi, le workflow relit la ligne Sheets. Si statut "Ferme", toute la sequence restante s'arrete. Aucun email envoye aux clients existants.
